关于金宝山水鸭

Why Kampar？

金宝山水鸭：寻山问水，铸就纯正品质

金宝山水鸭致力于提供最高标准的天然生态鲜鸭。我们深信「得有山又有水，鸭子才有好油水。」的传统智慧，因此坚持让鸭群远离拥挤的商业圈养，在清泉流淌、自然开阔的环境中自由放养。这种原生态的生长模式，造就了鸭肉紧实细嫩的口感与分布均匀的黄金皮下脂肪，使其成为专业烧腊师傅与高级餐厅不可或缺的顶级食材。

为了确保每一份食材都能达到食品行业的最高标准，金宝山水鸭坚持源头直供，直接对标业内最值得信赖的批发供应商体系。我们深知新鲜、安全的食材是餐饮业成功的基石，因此每一批山水鸭都经过精心筛选，确保交到零售商、餐饮服务商和家庭顾客手中的产品，始终保持着卓越的新鲜度与一致性。

Kampar Free Range Duck is committed to the highest standard of naturally raised fresh duck. We hold to the traditional wisdom that good mountains and good water make good duck, which is why our ducks are never kept in crowded commercial pens — instead, they roam freely across open, spring-fed surroundings. This natural rearing method produces duck with firm, tender meat and an even layer of golden fat beneath the skin, making it a prized ingredient for professional roast duck chefs and fine restaurants.

To ensure every batch meets the highest standards in the food industry, Kampar Free Range Duck supplies directly from the source, positioning ourselves among the most trusted names in the wholesale supply chain. We know that fresh, safe ingredients are the foundation of a successful food business, so every batch is carefully selected — ensuring retailers, foodservice partners and home customers alike receive duck of consistently excellent freshness and quality.

Duck Farming

自然放养，用心养成

金宝山水鸭坚持传统放养方式，鸭苗从孵化起便在开阔的山水环境中自由活动，不设围栏拥挤圈养。鸭群每日可自由走动、戏水，充分的活动空间让它们保持健康的体态与结实的肌肉，也让鸭肉的口感更加紧实弹牙。

除了自然环境，我们也为鸭群搭配专属饲料配方，确保营养均衡、生长稳定。从鸭苗到成鸭，整个养殖周期约50天以上，我们全程关注鸭群的健康与生长环境，确保每一批交付的山水鸭都维持一致的品质与新鲜度。

我们的鸭场坐落于霹雳金宝(Kampar)，我们从产地直接配送，服务吉隆坡(KL)、怡保(Ipoh)、金宝(Kampar)与雪兰莪(Selangor)全区的零售与批发客户。

紧实细嫩的肉质与均匀分布的皮下脂肪，让金宝山水鸭特别适合各种经典料理，例如烧鸭、卤鸭、姜母鸭与潮州卤水鸭，无论是家庭烹饪或餐饮业者的招牌菜，都能呈现皮脆肉嫩、锁汁多汁的效果。

Kampar Free Range Duck follows traditional free-range farming. From the moment they hatch, our ducklings roam freely across open hills and waterways, never confined to crowded pens. With daily space to walk and swim, the flock stays healthy and well-muscled — giving the meat its firm, springy texture.

Beyond the natural environment, we also use a dedicated feed formula to keep nutrition balanced and growth steady. From duckling to maturity, the full rearing cycle takes 50+ days, during which we closely monitor the flock's health and living conditions — ensuring every batch we deliver maintains the same quality and freshness.

Our farm is based in Kampar, Perak, and we deliver directly from the source to retail and wholesale customers across Kuala Lumpur (KL), Ipoh, Kampar and Selangor.

The firm, tender meat and evenly distributed fat under the skin make Kampar Free Range Duck ideal for classic dishes such as roast duck, braised duck, ginger duck and Teochew braised duck — delivering crispy skin, tender meat and juicy results, whether cooked at home or served as a restaurant signature dish.
