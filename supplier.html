Frequently Asked Questions
什么是山水鸭？ What is free-range duck?

山水鸭是指自然放养的鸭子——鸭苗从孵化起便在开阔的山林水域间自由活动、觅食与戏水，不被关笼。我们的鸭子在金宝自然放养，养殖周期约50天以上。

Free-range duck is raised uncaged — from hatching, the ducks roam, forage and swim freely across open hills and waterways. Ours are raised free-range in Kampar over a growing cycle of 50+ days.
